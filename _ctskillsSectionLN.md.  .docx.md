## **Review of Computational Thinking Skills**

**Study Guide**

### **Introduction**

In your Computer Science Foundation Years Program (FYP), you were introduced to the core principles of computational thinking and applied them through a programming language like python, c++ , javascript or java, that enabled you to build simple applications and projects. During this stage, you learned how to:

* Identify and define problems.  
* Break down complex tasks into smaller, manageable components (problem decomposition).  
* Recognize recurring structures and solutions (pattern recognition).  
* Represent information effectively (data representation).  
* Develop step-by-step solutions (algorithm design).

You also gained hands-on experience with data structures such as arrays, lists, dictionaries, objects and JSON, which allowed you to organize and manipulate information more efficiently.

Part of the FYP course, also emphasized what are the factors that are followed in order to have good and efficient solutions or codes.

In addition, you were introduced to essential program development tools:

* The Integrated Development Environment (IDE), which streamlines coding and debugging.  
* The concept of version control, an important practice for collaboration and teamwork in application development projects.

These tools were reintroduced to you in the first study guide.

In this module, that will cover four face to face meetings and 2 ILPs, you will be provided with resources, exercises, and review materials to help you revisit and strengthen the foundational skills learned in your CS1 and CS2 classes. 

### **Module Learning Objectives**

After working on this module, you should be able to:

1. Identify the smaller components of a given complex problem using decomposition.  
2. Design an algorithm using a flowchart or pseudocode to solve a specific problem.   
3. Evaluate the efficiency of a designed algorithm by identifying potential improvements and justifying modifications

The next section will give a summary of each learning resource and the requirements for each activity as outlined in the Study Schedule.

#### **Learning resource 1: Computational Thinking Skills**

***1.1. A Review of Computational Thinking Skills and Problem Decomposition***

One of the elements of Computational Thinking Skills is Problem decomposition.  It is the process of breaking down a complex problem into smaller, more manageable parts. Learning this skill, you will be able to tackle large coding challenges by identifying individual components that can be solved independently and then you can combine them into one complete solution.
